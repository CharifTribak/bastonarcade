DerbyTrace the shapes to make your horse gallop

Horse race: everyone gets the same series of shapes to trace. Each shape you complete moves your horse forward, further if it is complex and well traced. First past the line wins.

Play Derby

Controls

  • On a phone: Trace over the shape in one stroke with your finger, starting from the green dot.
  • On a computer: Same with the mouse: click the green dot and trace without letting go.

Tip

Stay in the middle of the blue band: the cleaner the line, the further your horse goes.

How to play with friends

Open Baston Arcade, choose "Play with others" then "Create a room". Your friends scan the QR code or type the room code on their phone. No account, no app to install, no ads. On your own, you play against robots.
