Dahi HandiHold your level, break the pot

▶ Play now👥 With friends

A co-op game inspired by Dahi Handi, the Indian festival where teams build a human pyramid to break a hanging pot. Each player holds one level: the ball of your spirit level slides off to one side on its own, bring it back to the middle with the left and right buttons. As long as every level is straight, the little climber goes up. If he reaches the top, he breaks the pot and the team scores. If a single level leans too far, the whole pyramid comes down and you start again. The crowd throws water at you, the wind blows in gusts, and every pot broken makes the next one harder. Free levels are held by robots.

Play Dahi Handi

Controls

  • On a phone: Hold the ◀ or ▶ button to bring your spirit level's ball back to the middle. Let go as soon as it is there.
  • On a computer: ← → or A D.

Watch how to play

Tip

Your level has a white dotted outline. It leans by itself, and even more when the level below leans or when the climber is on you. Watch out for the crowd's buckets of water, and when the wind is announced, push against it!

How to play with friends

Open Baston Arcade, choose "Play with others" then "Create a room". Your friends scan the QR code or type the room code on their phone. No account, no app to install, no ads. On your own, you play against robots.
