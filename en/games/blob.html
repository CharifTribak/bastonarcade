BlobDraw, eat the flakes, invade the others

The slime mould, that odd creature that wakes up in a Petri dish: every player has one, in the same dish. Draw a path from your blob with your finger and it grows along it, spending energy. Draw into another blob and you invade it, which costs more, the more energy it has. Your energy comes back slowly, the more slowly the bigger your blob, and not at all past half the dish: then you need big oat flakes, which speed it up for as long as you cover them. The lab assistant adds some now and then, anywhere. Crumbs are eaten in one go. A piece cut off from the rest of your blob, with no flake, withers. Tap your blob to place a reinforcement that hardens over time. After 4 minutes the biggest blob wins. Robots fill in when you play alone or in twos.

Play Blob

Controls

  • On a phone: Slide your finger from your blob to draw where it should grow, into another blob to attack it. Tap your blob to place a reinforcement.
  • On a computer: Same with the mouse: drag to draw, click your blob to reinforce.

Tip

Past half the dish, only the food from flakes keeps you going: steal the others'. Cut an opponent in two, the small piece with no flake withers on its own.

How to play with friends

Open Baston Arcade, choose "Play with others" then "Create a room". Your friends scan the QR code or type the room code on their phone. No account, no app to install, no ads. On your own, you play against robots.
