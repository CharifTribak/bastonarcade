MedusaThe highest one waves at the Argus

The Raft of the Medusa, comic-book style: you build the painting's pyramid of bodies yourselves. For two minutes, everyone drops castaways onto the raft whenever they like, one every three seconds. The hanging body swings from standing to lying: you pick the moment. Bodies slide, the raft rocks, some fall into the sea and are gone. Your highest castaway, once settled, scores for as long as it stays on top; three times the Argus passes on the horizon, and the top is then worth five times as much. Dropping a body on the leader's head to knock it off is part of the game. Robots fill in when you play alone or in twos.

Play Medusa

Controls

  • On a phone: Slide your finger above the raft to aim, let go to drop your castaway.
  • On a computer: Aim with the mouse and click, or ← → then Space.

Tip

Lying, a body holds better; standing, it goes higher but topples. The Argus passes three times: be on top then, it is worth 5 times as much.

How to play with friends

Open Baston Arcade, choose "Play with others" then "Create a room". Your friends scan the QR code or type the room code on their phone. No account, no app to install, no ads. On your own, you play against robots.
