Musical chairsThe party classic, on your phone

Musical chairs: the music plays, the record spins, everyone walks around. When the record stops, quickly tap a free chair: there is one fewer than players. Whoever is left standing loses a heart. Beware of fake stops: while the record is spinning, touching a chair costs a penalty. The last one in wins.

Play Musical chairs

Controls

  • On a phone: When the record stops, quickly tap a free chair.
  • On a computer: When the record stops, quickly click a free chair.

Tip

Tapping a chair while the music plays costs a 1 s penalty. Watch out for fake stops!

How to play with friends

Open Baston Arcade, choose "Play with others" then "Create a room". Your friends scan the QR code or type the room code on their phone. No account, no app to install, no ads. On your own, you play against robots.
